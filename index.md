# Privacy Policy for Private AI Journal

_Last updated: June 17, 2026_

Private AI Journal is a local-first journaling app built to keep your writing, recordings, photos, and reflections private. This policy explains what the app does and does not collect.

## What We Collect

Private AI Journal does not collect, transmit, store on any server, sell, or share your personal information.

The app has:

- No user accounts
- No custom backend
- No analytics SDK
- No advertising SDK
- No third-party trackers
- No third-party AI service

## What Stays On Your Device

Private AI Journal stores your journal data locally on your device. This can include:

- Journal entries
- Audio recordings
- Transcripts
- Photos you choose to attach or include
- Journals, tags, moods, colors, and favorites
- Local search and reflection history
- Export files you create

This data stays on your device unless you choose to export it or share it yourself.

## On-Device AI

Private AI Journal uses Apple on-device intelligence features when they are available on your device. These features can help with transcript cleanup, entry organization, title suggestions, recaps, and reflections grounded in your own journal.

Private AI Journal does not send your journal content to the developer, to a custom server, or to any third-party AI provider.

## Optional Device Permissions

Private AI Journal may ask for permission to access device features only when you choose to use related functionality:

- **Microphone**: to record audio journal entries.
- **Speech recognition**: to transcribe audio locally when supported.
- **Photo Library**: to attach photos or suggest moments from photos you choose to include.
- **Calendar and Reminders**: to suggest moments for "Write My Day" from events or reminders on your device.
- **Health**: to include summaries such as workouts, steps, or sleep when you choose to use them in "Write My Day".
- **Location**: to save your current place as private entry metadata when you choose to include it.
- **Face ID or device passcode**: to lock the app on your device.

These permissions are optional and can be managed in iOS Settings.

## Exports

If you use Private AI Journal's export feature, the app creates files on your device in formats such as Markdown, JSON, or ZIP. You control where those exported files go.

## Changes To This Policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated.
